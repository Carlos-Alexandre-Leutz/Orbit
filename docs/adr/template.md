# ADR-NNN: Title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? Include the constraints that limit the choice.

## Decision

What was decided, in plain sentences. Say what is true today and what is only planned or still open.

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what risk the decision accepts.

---

How to use this template: copy it to `docs/adr/ADR-NNN-short-title.md`, number it with the next free number and never renumber. To change a decision, write a new ADR and mark the old one `Superseded by ADR-NNN`; do not rewrite history. Keep ADRs short enough to read in a few minutes.
